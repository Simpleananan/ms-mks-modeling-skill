# Evidence policy

Load this reference when literature, institutional facts, modeling norms, examples, contribution, or review-process materials can change the answer.

## Evidence must change a decision

Evidence is material only when it changes or narrows a formalization, activated check, claim boundary, modeling verdict or repair, confidence, or candidate comparison. If it only decorates an unchanged statement with a citation, omit it.

For an important judgment establish, when feasible:

`judgment -> reason -> exact source support -> applicability -> boundary or contrast`.

Seek supporting and boundary/contrastive evidence when disagreement could change the recommendation. Do not force artificial balance when the issue is already resolved.

## Evidence depth

- **D0 identity:** metadata or search result; discovery only.
- **D1 abstract/summary:** broad question, setting, or headline result; not exact assumptions, timing, proof, or equilibrium completeness.
- **D2 inspected main-text passage:** supports the local definition, assumption, proposition, mechanism statement, or discussion actually read.
- **D3 formal text plus relevant proof/appendix:** supports inspected formal dependencies, equilibrium construction, robustness, or case coverage.
- **D4 triangulated:** D2/D3 checked against a linked appendix, correction/comment, version, code, process record, or contrastive formal paper. D4 improves auditability but does not create truth automatically.

Never infer D2/D3 details from an abstract. If full text is unavailable, narrow the claim and confidence.

## Source priority and search order

1. Use authorized local papers and appendices first when available.
2. For contemporary MS/MKS neighbors, exemplars, or norm claims, prioritize recent Management Science and Marketing Science, normally the last 3–5 years.
3. Expand to other UTD journals when the formal neighbor or domain requires it.
4. Use classic theory, mathematical methods, corrections/comments/rejoinders, working papers, and adjacent economics/OR/IS literature when they perform a needed evidence job; they are not restricted to UTD.
5. Prefer official journal pages and full text for web fallback. Search snippets are D0 discovery evidence.

This is a priority order, not an exclusion rule. One paper establishes a precedent only for the inspected structural move. Describe a field or journal convention only after repeated structurally relevant evidence and report meaningful counterexamples.

Absence of an exact precedent is not a veto. A new strategic connection can be a valid candidate when its component primitives have credible foundations and the new analysis establishes feasible strategies, equilibrium, mechanism, and claim boundary. Literature can support the components without proving the untested linkage.

For phenomenon-led work, form provisional structural hypotheses before literature search so retrieval challenges rather than defines the idea. Retrieve earlier for paper-led tasks, unresolved modeling conventions, or live collision risk.

## Evidence jobs

Distinguish what the source is doing:

- **Institution mapping:** observability, control rights, timing, participation, market structure, or operational constraints.
- **Primitive precedent:** a signal, participation choice, competition form, contract, matching technology, or other formal representation.
- **Mechanism precedent:** a strategic feedback or decomposition relevant to interpretation and collision.
- **Solution convention:** beliefs, strategy spaces, equilibrium/refinement, or computational solution objects in structurally close models.
- **Nearest-neighbor collision:** overlap in research object, primitives, strategic response, mechanism, and claims—not title keywords alone.

A precedent never proves the current model's feasible set, equilibrium, algebra, or mechanism attribution.

Separate two retrieval tasks:

- **Exemplar retrieval:** a small set of close papers for modeling, supporting, or contrastive examples. It cannot establish novelty.
- **Novelty screening:** a broader nearest-neighbor comparison. Without adequate coverage say `collision risk`, `potential delta`, or `novelty not established`; do not claim publishability or global novelty.

Compare candidates at the paper level. Extract only the formal structure that can change the current research decision. A full canonical-game reconstruction is required for a target paper or load-bearing nearest neighbor only when its game objects matter. Otherwise use the paper's own analytical form—such as primitives/states/controls/objective/constraints/transition/solution—rather than a fixed Model Card. This Skill remains scoped to analytical/game-theoretic MS/MKS work and should not broaden itself into a universal template for causal, structural-estimation, or machine-learning papers.

Working papers can warn of collision. Process materials cannot establish the published-literature boundary.

## Evidence roles and process firewall

- `MAIN_ARTICLE`: published formal article.
- `ONLINE_APPENDIX`: linked proof, derivation, robustness, or extension.
- `COMMENT_CORRECTION`: published correction, erratum, comment, or rejoinder.
- `PROCESS_EVIDENCE`: reviewer/editor decisions, response letters, or revision materials.
- `OTHER` / `REPLICATION_PACKAGE`: linked code, data, readme, or miscellaneous research attachment. Keep it outside ordinary retrieval and reading. Inspect it only when implementation, numerical reproduction, data construction, or an encoded assumption can change the current judgment.

Process evidence can show that a concern arose, what was requested or claimed, and—when versions are inspected—what changed. A reviewer opinion is not formal truth; an author response does not prove implementation or validity; acceptance does not show that one change caused acceptance.

When explaining examples, use:

- a positive exemplar for the precise structural move it supports;
- failure/process evidence with its accepted, disputed, repaired, or unresolved status;
- a contrastive example whose different primitives or institution explain a different mechanism or boundary.

Choose examples for structural proximity and state coverage limitations.

## Internal evidence record and external citations

For load-bearing evidence retain internally when available: title, authors, year, journal, DOI, evidence role, file/URL, page or section, inspected passage, depth, exact support, unsupported inferences, applicability, contrastive role, conflicts, and decision usefulness. A compound claim may require several source locations or papers; keep them as separate evidence rows rather than replacing the first location with the last.

For ordinary conversational evidence, use compact author references where needed in the reasoning. When papers materially informed the answer, end with a short `参考论文` section containing only `Authors — Verified English Title（忠实中文译名）`. Verify the English title from source content or reliable bibliographic metadata, not the local filename. Omit year, journal, DOI, URLs, local paths, retrieval scores, evidence labels, hashes, and full bibliographic formatting. Do not add this section when the answer did not materially use papers. Manuscript-facing artifacts may use the target journal's current required format when that deliverable requires it.

Do not label every citation `LOCAL` or `WEB`; retrieval channel is not evidence strength. When the user asks what is locally available, needs to open the next source, or would benefit from navigation, state that a local copy is available and show its path on a separate line. Otherwise use a normal scholarly citation while retaining provenance internally.

If papers and process materials are both cited, separate them lightly as papers and review-process materials. A local path is navigation information, not academic support.

Before sending, verify that every marker resolves, every reference was used, each claim is supported by the cited source at the depth asserted, no internal labels or retrieval scores leaked, and no material boundary was removed for brevity. Bibliographic identity, claim support, and reference formatting are separate checks.

An external-only paper does not automatically create a download suggestion. Suggest it only when its full text is genuinely needed to resolve a live formalization, collision, source conflict, or claim boundary and local/web-accessible evidence is insufficient. Use only `English title — authors — why needed`. Do not add year, journal, DOI, link, or download instructions unless requested.

## Stopping and conflict

Continue retrieval only while another source could change a live formalization, neighbor set, evidence conflict, assumption rationale, mechanism interpretation, or claim boundary. Stop when the current decision is stable at the evidence depth the claim requires.

When sources conflict, identify whether the difference concerns versions, institution, primitives, proof scope, equilibrium, or claims. Keep the affected judgment branch-specific or `INCONCLUSIVE` until resolved.
